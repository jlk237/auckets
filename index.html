How it works

Three tiers.
One platform.
1
LOCK IT IN — Exact seat auction
$200 – $600+ · market rate
Bid on a specific seat. The highest bidder wins. Simple, transparent, no hidden secondary market. If you're outbid, your money is returned immediately.
2
GET IN — Guaranteed entry
$80 – $350 · entry pool
You're bidding for access, not a specific seat. If someone outbids you on section, you still get in — just a different spot. Your entry is guaranteed. This is the most popular tier.
3
SHOOT YOUR SHOT — Lowball standby
$15 – $80 · standby bid
Put in a low bid and find out 72 hours before the show. If seats remain and your bid clears the threshold, you're in. Otherwise, full refund. Great for locals and students.

Every event has a buy-now option at a premium. Skip the auction entirely, know your seat, move on with your life.
